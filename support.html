Support

How can we help?

Quick answers are below. For anything else, write to ianshinvl@gmail.com. We read every email and usually reply within a couple of days.

Email support

Hide, pause, quit

Everything lives in the menu bar icon at the top right of your screen.

  • Hide: click the menu bar icon and choose Hide. Choose Show to bring your friend back.
  • Pause: choose Pause to freeze your friend in place. Choose Resume to carry on.
  • Quit: choose Quit from the same menu. Cozyling stops completely and your pet is saved.

Launch at login

Off by default. Turn it on or off in Settings inside the app, or in System Settings, General, Login Items.

I cannot see my pet

  • Click the menu bar icon and check that it says Hide, not Show, and that the app is not paused.
  • Full-screen apps hide your friend by default. You can change that in Settings.
  • Try dragging an open window to another spot. Your friend might be napping on the far side of a window.
  • Still stuck? Quit and reopen the app. Your pet is saved automatically.

Uninstall

  1. Choose Quit from the menu bar icon.
  2. Open Applications in Finder and drag Cozyling to the Trash.
  3. Optional: your pet's saved data lives in ~/Library/Containers/app.cozyling.mac. Move that folder to the Trash too if you want a clean slate.

You can reinstall from the Mac App Store at any time.

Privacy

Cozyling has no network access and collects no data. See the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Does it block my clicks?

No. Clicks pass straight through to the apps underneath. The only places that catch them are the pet's own painted pixels and the little things you can pick up, like toys and furniture. It never steals keyboard focus either.

Will it use my CPU?

Hardly any. Cozyling draws at full speed only while something is moving. When your friend is idle or asleep it slows right down, and it draws nothing while hidden or while a full-screen app is in front.

Does it need permissions?

No. There is no Accessibility, Screen Recording or Input Monitoring request, and no pop-ups asking for access. To walk along your windows it reads only their public position and size, never their titles or what is inside them.

Is it free?

It is a one-time purchase of $5.99 from the Mac App Store. No subscription, no ads, no account, and nothing you have to keep paying to keep your friend.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 13 or later: Ventura and newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Is there a Windows version?

Cozyling is a Mac app. There is no Windows version.

What about multiple displays?

Cozyling knows about every display you have connected, so your friend can live wherever your windows are.

What happens in full-screen apps?

By default your friend steps out of the way and stops drawing while a full-screen app is active. You can change that in Settings.

Can I hide it?

Any time. The menu bar icon lets you hide, pause or quit in one click. Launch at login is optional and off until you switch it on.

What if I forget about it for a week?

Nothing bad happens, ever. There is no death, no guilt and no game over. If its needs run low your friend just looks sleepy or a little sad, and a snack fixes everything.

Is my data private?

Completely. The app has no network access, no analytics and no account. Everything it remembers stays on your Mac.
