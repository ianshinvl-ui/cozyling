How it works

Four steps. No homework.

  1. Choose an egg

    Pick a species and a colour. Every egg is a little different.

  2. Keep it warm

    Pet it and keep it company. When it is warm enough, it hatches.

  3. Raise it

    Feed it, wash it, tidy up after it. It grows while you work.

  4. Play together

    Toys, mini-games, hats and furniture. Small cozy rituals.

Lives on your screen

It walks your windows. Not your nerves.

Cozyling sits quietly above your desktop and moves around the things you already have open.

  • Walks, jumps and climbs. It strolls on title bars, hops between windows and scrambles up their edges.
  • Clicks pass straight through. Only the pet's own painted pixels catch the mouse. It never steals focus.
  • Steps aside for full-screen apps. It stops drawing and waits quietly until you are back.
  • Yours to control. Hide, pause or quit from the menu bar whenever you like.

Care, cleanup and bath

Yes, it poops. It is very cute about it.

Looking after a friend means a little housekeeping. We made it gentle, funny and quick, and never punishing.

Feed and play

Open the care panel from the menu bar icon. Pick a snack or a toy and your friend gets stuck in. Favourite foods get a bigger smile.

Little swirls

After a meal your friend strains, blushes, and leaves a tiny swirl. Click it and it goes pop and pays you a coin. Leave it and things get a bit smelly, with a fly or two. Never sick, never hurt.

Bath time

Your cursor becomes a sponge. Scrub until it is all bubbles, then watch it shake dry with a shower of droplets. Calm and oddly satisfying.

Private, tiny, yours

Your Mac stays your Mac.

No account, no ads and no analytics. Everything your friend remembers stays on your computer.

No network

The app has no network access at all, so nothing can leave your Mac. It works offline because it never goes online.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no telemetry. This website does not use cookies or third-party scripts either.

Questions

Good questions.

Does it block my clicks?

No. Clicks pass straight through to the apps underneath. The only places that catch them are the pet's own painted pixels and the little things you can pick up, like toys and furniture. It never steals keyboard focus either.

Will it use my CPU?

Hardly any. Cozyling draws at full speed only while something is moving. When your friend is idle or asleep it slows right down, and it draws nothing while hidden or while a full-screen app is in front.

Does it need permissions?

No. There is no Accessibility, Screen Recording or Input Monitoring request, and no pop-ups asking for access. To walk along your windows it reads only their public position and size, never their titles or what is inside them.

Is it free?

It is a one-time purchase of $5.99 from the Microsoft Store (Windows) or, soon, the Mac App Store. No subscription, no ads, no account, and nothing you have to keep paying to keep your friend.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 13 or later: Ventura and newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. Cozyling for Windows 10 and 11 is out now in the Microsoft Store. The Mac version is coming soon to the Mac App Store.

What about multiple displays?

Cozyling knows about every display you have connected, so your friend can live wherever your windows are.

What happens in full-screen apps?

By default your friend steps out of the way and stops drawing while a full-screen app is active. You can change that in Settings.

Can I hide it?

Any time. The menu bar icon lets you hide, pause or quit in one click. Launch at login is optional and off until you switch it on.

What if I forget about it for a week?

Nothing bad happens, ever. There is no death, no guilt and no game over. If its needs run low your friend just looks sleepy or a little sad, and a snack fixes everything.

Is my data private?

Completely. The app has no network access, no analytics and no account. Everything it remembers stays on your Mac.
