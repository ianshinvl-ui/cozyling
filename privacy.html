Privacy policy

The short version: Cozyling does not collect any data about you. Nothing leaves your Mac or your PC.

Data not collected. No account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking, no network connection.

The Cozyling app

What stays on your Mac

Your pet's name, species, colour, age, stats, coins, items and settings are saved on your Mac, inside the app's own sandbox container, so the pet is still there next time you open the app. This data is never sent anywhere, because the app has no network access at all. Deleting the app and its container removes it.

No network

The app contains no networking code and is not granted the network entitlement. It cannot send or receive anything over the internet, and it works the same way offline.

No special permissions

Cozyling never asks for Accessibility, Screen Recording, Input Monitoring, Contacts, Files, Camera, Microphone or Location access.

What it looks at, and what it does not

  • To let your pet walk along your windows, the app reads the position and size of the windows on your screen through a public macOS interface. It does not read window titles, the names of other apps or anything inside a window, and it does not record or capture your screen. These numbers are used on the spot and are not stored or transmitted.
  • To know when you are away (so your pet can nap), the app can ask macOS how long it has been since your last mouse or keyboard activity. That is a single number. The app never sees what you type or click.
  • The app only uses your mouse position to react to it, for example to look at your cursor or to scrub during a bath. It is not saved.

Launch at login

This is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, the app uses the standard macOS login item feature. You can turn it off in Settings or in System Settings, General, Login Items.

Purchases

Cozyling is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment and shows us only aggregate sales and crash reports in the way Apple's own policies describe, and we never receive your name, address or payment details.

Cozyling for Windows

Cozyling for Windows works fully offline. It has no network access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry.

To let your pet walk on top of windows, the app reads only the position and size of open windows and the taskbar. It never captures the screen and never reads what is inside other windows.

Your pet, settings and progress are saved only on your PC, in the folder %APPDATA%\Cozyling. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes all of its data.

The Windows version is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles payment, and we never receive your name, address or payment details.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Everything is served from the same place. If you choose a light or dark theme with the toggle in the header, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage on your device so the page remembers it. It is never sent to us.

The hosting provider that serves the site may keep standard server logs for a short time for security and reliability. We do not use them to identify visitors.

Email

If you email cozyling.official@gmail.com, we receive your address and message. We use them only to reply, and we do not share them with anyone. Ask us to delete them at any time.

Children

Cozyling collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes we will update this page and the date at the top. If we ever added something that collected data, we would say so here and in the app before shipping it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to cozyling.official@gmail.com.
